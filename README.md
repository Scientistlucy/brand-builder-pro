## The Prompt (Copy & Paste into Lovable)

```

Build a complete, production-ready website for "Rahisi Movers" — a premium moving and relocation company based in Nairobi, Kenya.

## DESIGN DIRECTION

**Aesthetic:** "Editorial Industrial" — a bold fusion of high-contrast editorial magazine styling with industrial logistics authenticity. Think black-and-white photography, oversized typography, modular layouts, and campaign-like visual rhythm.

**Tone:** Confident, trustworthy, and effortlessly premium. Not flashy — quietly authoritative. The kind of website that makes clients feel they're in safe hands before they even pick up the phone.

**Differentiation Anchor:** If the logo were removed, someone would recognize this site by its bold black-and-white visual language, oversized typographic statements, and editorial-quality imagery that feels more like a magazine feature than a logistics brochure.

## VISUAL SYSTEM

**Typography:**

- Display font: "Bebas Neue" or "Anton" — bold, compact, high-impact for headlines and campaign statements

- Body font: "Inter" or "Work Sans" — clean, highly readable, modern

- Scale: Dramatic contrast between massive hero headlines (4-6rem) and refined body text

**Color Palette (CSS Variables):**

```

--color-primary: #1A1A1A (near-black for backgrounds and headlines)

--color-secondary: #FFFFFF (pure white for text on dark)

--color-accent: #D4A84B (warm gold — subtle, premium accent for CTAs and highlights)

--color-gray-100: #F5F5F5

--color-gray-300: #D1D1D1

--color-gray-500: #8A8A8A

--color-gray-700: #4A4A4A

--color-gray-900: #1A1A1A

```

**Spatial Composition:**

- Asymmetric, editorial-style layouts with intentional white space

- Full-bleed hero with oversized typography overlapping imagery

- Modular grid sections that break the standard template rhythm

- Generous padding and breathing room — nothing feels cramped

**Motion Philosophy:**

- Sparse, purposeful, high-impact

- One strong entrance sequence (hero text reveal on load)

- Subtle hover states on cards and CTAs

- No decorative micro-animations

- Scroll-triggered reveals for key sections

**Texture & Depth:**

- Subtle grain/noise overlay on hero section (CSS generated)

- High-contrast black-and-white or desaturated imagery

- Clean, sharp borders — no rounded corners

- Minimal shadows with editorial intent

## WEBSITE STRUCTURE

### 1. Hero Section

- Full-screen or near-full-screen dark background

- Massive headline: "Move with Confidence." or "Your Journey, Our Precision."

- Sub-headline: "Local & International Relocation — Nairobi, Kenya"

- Primary CTA: "Get a Free Quote" (gold accent button)

- Secondary CTA: "Call Now: +254 XXX XXX XXX"

- Professional imagery of a moving truck or team in action (use placeholder images)

### 2. Trust Bar

- Logos/badges: Google Reviews stars, "Licensed & Insured," "5+ Years Experience"

- Key metric counters: "500+ Moves Completed," "100% Client Satisfaction"

- Displayed in a clean, horizontal strip

### 3. Services Section

Three service cards with icons:

- **Residential Moving** — House to house, apartment moves, packing services

- **Office Relocation** — Business moves, IT equipment, minimal downtime

- **International Moving** — Cross-border relocation, customs clearance

Each card: Icon + Title + Brief description + "Learn More" link

### 4. Why Choose Rahisi Movers

3-4 pillars with short explanations:

- **Reliable & Punctual** — We arrive on time, every time

- **Careful Handling** — Zero-scratch packing system for your valuables

- **Transparent Pricing** — No hidden fees, clear quotes upfront

- **Experienced Team** — Trained professionals you can trust

### 5. Testimonials / Social Proof

- 3-4 client testimonials with names and locations

- Google review stars displayed prominently

- "Highly recommend — amazing experience from start to finish!"

### 6. Instant Quote Calculator

A simple, 3-step form:

- **Step 1:** Move type (Residential / Office / International)

- **Step 2:** From → To (locations)

- **Step 3:** Approximate size (Studio / 1BR / 2BR / 3BR+ / Office)

- Output: Estimated price range + "Get Detailed Quote" button

### 7. Service Area

Display: Nairobi, Mombasa, and major Kenyan cities + international destinations

### 8. Footer

- Company name, tagline

- Contact: Phone, Email, Address

- Quick links: Services, About, Contact, Privacy Policy

- Social media icons (placeholder)

## IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

**Tech Stack:**

- React + TypeScript (or plain HTML/CSS/JS with modern CSS)

- CSS Modules or styled-components for scoped styling

- Framer Motion for scroll-triggered animations (if React)

**Code Quality:**

- Semantic HTML5

- Fully responsive (mobile-first)

- WCAG 2.1 AA accessible (contrast, keyboard navigation, focus states)

- Performance-optimized (lazy loading, compressed assets)

- No dead styles, no unused animations

**Accessibility:**

- Minimum contrast ratio: 4.5:1 for text

- All interactive elements keyboard-navigable

- ARIA labels where needed

- Focus indicators on all interactive elements

## ANTI-PATTERNS TO AVOID

❌ Purple-on-white SaaS gradients

❌ Generic stock photos of smiling people in offices

❌ Symmetrical, predictable "template" layouts

❌ Inter/Roboto/Arial as the only fonts

❌ Decorative micro-animations everywhere

❌ Cluttered, busy sections

❌ Default Bootstrap/Tailwind component styling

## CONTENT PLACEHOLDERS

Use these where specific content isn't provided:

With a commitment to punctuality, care, and transparent pricing, we've helped hundreds of families and businesses transition to new spaces — locally and internationally."

**Tagline Options:** "Move with Confidence." | "Your Journey, Our Precision." | "Relocation, Redefined."

**Testimonials (use as placeholders):**

- "Amazing experience, from the movers to the team who helped us throughout the process! Highly recommend." — Client, Nairobi

- "Professional, careful, and on time. Will definitely use them again." — Client, Mombasa

Generate:

2. All CSS with CSS variables for the design system

3. Responsive breakpoints for mobile, tablet, and desktop

4. Functional quote calculator with basic logic

5. Smooth, purposeful animations

6. Comments only where design intent isn't obvious

```

## Why This Prompt Works

| Element | How It Avoids "AI Generic" |

|---------|---------------------------|

| **Editorial Industrial aesthetic** | Named, intentional design stance — not "modern clean" |

| **Black & white + gold accent** | High-contrast, memorable palette — not purple SaaS |

| **Oversized typography** | Structural, campaign-like — not safe body text |

| **Asymmetric modular layouts** | Breaks the grid — not symmetrical template blocks |

| **Bebas Neue / Anton** | Expressive display font — not Inter/Roboto default |

| **Sparse, purposeful motion** | One big entrance, not micro-motion spam |

| **Quote calculator** | Functional conversion tool — not just a contact form |

| **Trust signals + social proof** | Builds credibility instantly |
